House Ultimates

A comeback move for players who are falling behind. Spend three cards to unleash your House's signature power on the leading player or alliance. Every number on this sheet is benchmarked against match 5METH, where The Quiet One snowballed to 54 reinforcements a turn while its allies sat on 6 and 23. v5 below is your balance notes (v3 and v4), replayed on the same 1,000 simulated games as v4. Everything that changed from v4 is marked v5.

Leaders who get targeted are usually the strongest ones, so they drift up a little faster than +2.2 even without a cast.

  • Walk-outs caused the long games, not the lock. Letting the lock expire didn't help, because bots could walk out and cast again a round later. Barring deserters from Ultimates for 3 rounds stopped walk-outs and brought Siege and Olympus back to control levels.
  • Without walk-outs, Ultimates barely change who wins. The round-4 leader is among the winners in 55% of games, against 57% with Ultimates off. Most of v4's drop to 42% came from broken alliances (solo wins rose from 4% to 11%), not from the Ultimates themselves.

How Ultimates work

These rules were agreed in v4, except where a card is marked v5. They control who can cast an Ultimate and when.

SettingHouse Ultimates on/offOn by default for 3+ players, off for 2-player games.
UnlocksRound 4After 3 full rounds, counted by a real round counter (not total turns).
Who can castBottom half of partiesRanked by Win %. An alliance counts as one party with its members' scores added. Checked at the start of your turn.
Win %40% territory · 40% armies · 20% PvP winsEach factor is your share of all living players' total. A defensive win counts once per territory per enemy turn.
CostAny 3 cards, 1+ from your birth HouseReplaces your trade (no +10 armies) and counts as your forced trade at 5 cards.
Cooldown3 of your own turnsPer player. Allies don't share a cooldown.
TargetsOne player, or a whole allianceAn alliance target hits every member, with each House's alliance version (about 60% strength each).
Alliance lockout v5Leave an alliance, locked out of all alliances for 2 turnsWalk out, answer a Rally or attack an ally, and you can't form or join any alliance for 2 of your turns. After that you can ally with anyone again. Replaces v4's permanent lock.
  • No direct knockouts. Damage never takes a territory below 1 army, and a House only falls when its Standard is taken.
  • Standards are safe from Ultimates. Where's Sevro?, Stormfall and Rot can't touch the territory holding a Standard, and Where's Sevro? can't seize a Keep. Only Diana goes after the Standard itself.
  • Effects over time tick at the start of the target's turn (Rot also ticks once on cast), and show as a counter on their banner so everyone can plan around them.
  • Casting happens in your Draft, in place of a trade. You still play the rest of your turn.

Status effects v5

Every lasting effect gets an icon on the player's banner, so anyone can see what's stacked on whom. The ring says what kind of effect it is, the dot says which House caused it, and the number is how many of the bearer's turns it has left. An icon with no number ends at the start of the caster's next turn.

The seven Ultimates

Grouped by role. Each one is built from mechanics the engine already has: direct kills, the Olympus-style countdown, reinforcement changes, dice modifiers and Standard guards.

Strikes
♂
House Mars · the Wolves

Where's Sevro?

Seize territory
Rules
Cast v5

Choose 3 territories held by a rival or by no one, in any quadrant where Mars holds at least one territory. You don't need to border them. On each, half the armies are destroyed (rounded down) and the rest join Mars: the territory becomes yours with those armies.

Limits

Never a Keep, never the territory holding a Standard, and never in a quadrant where Mars has no land. Against an alliance, the 3 picks can come from any of its members.

Power vs one player (swing)
≈37Round 4 · stacks of ~15, 12, 10: 18 destroyed, 19 join Mars, 3 territories
≈132Round 11 · stacks of 62, 40, 30: 66 destroyed, 66 join Mars, 3 territories
Desired outcome

Rip the leader's best stacks out from under them and turn them into a Mars army deep in enemy land. It's the only Ultimate that takes territory, so it lowers the leader's territory share and income, not just their army count.

Intended meta

No snipes across the map: Mars strikes where it already fights. To reach a new quadrant, blitz in first, or ride an ally's Wild Hunt (Long Strike lets the party jump a territory). Inside Mars's quadrants, only Keeps and Standard tiles are safe, so leaders pull their big stacks onto them. Seized armies land behind enemy lines and open a second front.

House theme

Mars is the war House, and its Howlers never come through the front door. Sevro's Wolfcloaks hide where no one would look and come out swinging inside the enemy's best ground.

“They were in the dead horses…”Cast prompt

Make it stronger
  • Allow 4 territories instead of 3.
  • Seized territories keep all their armies (none destroyed).
Make it weaker
  • A quarter joins Mars instead of half.
  • Seize only 2 territories.
  • Cap what joins Mars at 20 per territory.
The biggest swing on the sheet. Every army on a seized stack counts twice: once off the target, once onto Mars. Seat 2's 122-army Olympus stack in 5METH would have become a 61-army Mars stack in one pick.

SimThe quadrant rule cut casts by about a third (0.69 per seat-game as written, 0.38 with the deserter bar) and roughly halved the pull on the leader: the target is −4.2 Win % two rounds later as written and −2.4 with the bar (−6.9 and −4.8 before the rule). It still holds leaders back better than every other House except Minerva. A cast swings 33–42 armies on average, up to 185.

♃
House Jupiter · the Storm-Born

Stormfall

Quadrant strike
Rules
Cast v5

Choose one of the 4 quadrants. Every territory the target holds there with more than 5 armies is cut to 5. The Standard's territory is immune.

vs an alliance

If the target is in an alliance, every member's territories in that quadrant are cut to 5 as well.

Storm-bound v5

The targeted player's next Draft is capped at 5 reinforcements. Trades they make that turn still add on top. Other alliance members keep their Draft.

Power vs one target (sim)
38–48Average value per cast: about half off stacks, half off the Draft (with deserter bar · as written)
234Biggest single cast
Desired outcome

Flatten a whole front at once. Every stack in the quadrant drops to a garrison, so anyone nearby can break through, and a doom-stack staged for an invasion is gone.

Intended meta

Leaders can't mass armies in one region, which is where they usually mass them: their home quadrant or the Foot of Olympus. Spreading armies across quadrants, or keeping the big stack under the Standard, becomes the counterplay.

House theme

The Storm-Born strike everywhere at once. Thunderjaw's Thunderclap breaks one defensive line; Stormfall breaks all of them.

“I am the storm, you damp little shit.”Thunderjaw, Primus of Jupiter

Make it stronger
  • Cap every alliance member's Draft at 5, not just the target's.
  • The target can't Fortify into the quadrant next turn ("grounded by the storm").
Make it weaker
  • Cut to 10 instead of 5.
  • Cap the Draft at 10 instead of 5.

SimThe Draft cap fixed it. Bots now cast Stormfall about twice as often (0.89 per seat-game as written, 0.43 with the deserter bar), and the target no longer outgrows it: −1.1 Win % two rounds later as written and +0.6 with the bar, against +4.1 and +6.3 without the cap. About 19–23 armies a cast now come off the Draft, and 19–25 off stacks.

Damage over time
♇
House Pluto · the Jackals

Rot

Over 3 turns
Rules
vs one player v5

Three ticks on every territory with 5+ armies (rounded down): 30% the moment it's cast, 20% at the start of the target's next turn, 10% at the start of the turn after. A stack that drops under 5 stops rotting.

Draft rot v5

The target's next three Drafts are cut by the same amounts: 30%, then 20%, then 10%.

vs an alliance

Each member takes the first two ticks only: stacks and Drafts −30%, then −20%.

Power vs one player
≈24Round 4 · ~15 off stacks, plus 9 off Drafts (5 + 3 + 1)
≈115Round 11 · ~84 off stacks, plus 31 off Drafts (16 + 10 + 5)
Desired outcome

Make hoarding expensive. Every turn the target waits, their big stacks shrink, so they're forced to spend armies now.

Intended meta

The counter to turtling and to staging for a Siege. Cast it on an alliance that's massing at the Foot of Olympus. The target's best answer is to attack straight away, which pulls them out of position.

House theme

The Jackals win slowly, through poison, patience and betrayal. Rot is the House's whole personality as a status effect.

“The dead keep excellent secrets.”Proctor Pluto

Make it stronger
  • Lower the threshold to 3+.
  • Each tick, the rot spreads to one neighbouring territory of the target.
Make it weaker
  • Against an alliance, take only the first tick (30%).
  • Draft rot only on the first two Drafts.
  • Ends early if the target dominates a House ("fed the Jackal").

Economy and tempo
⌘
House Minerva · the Owls

Blackout

Stun
Rules
vs one player

The target is completely stunned on their next turn: no Draft (no reinforcements, trades or card plays), no attacks and no Fortify. The caster sees the target's entire hand.

vs an alliance

On their next turn, each member's Draft is cut by 60%, and they can't use cards, attack or Fortify. No hand reveal against an alliance.

Power vs one player
≈17Round 4 · a whole turn: Draft, attacks and Fortify
≈54Round 11 · The Quiet One's full turn-61 Draft, plus its whole turn
Desired outcome

Take away one entire turn from the leader, and give the caster a full read of their cards.

Intended meta

A timing weapon. Cast it the turn before the leader's big Draft: when they'll be forced to trade at 5 cards, or just before they can call a Siege. On turn 61 of 5METH it would have cost The Quiet One 54 armies and pushed the Siege back a round.

House theme

The Owls are spies and strategists. Quill runs the spy network and Mustang out-thinks whole armies. Blackout cuts the enemy's supply lines and leaves them blind.

“Wisdom is just cruelty with patience.”Proctor Minerva

Make it stronger
  • The caster sees the target's hand for the rest of the game.
  • The stun lasts 2 turns against a single player.
Make it weaker
  • Allow attacks, keep the rest of the stun.
  • Allow trades during the skipped Draft.
  • Against an alliance, members keep their full Draft.
Now a full skipped turn. Against a whole alliance it freezes every member for a round, which is the strongest alliance version on the sheet. Armies are never removed, though, so it still can't knock anyone out on its own.

SimUnchanged in v5. The target is −3.1 Win % two rounds later as written and −5.2 with the deserter bar. It's still the strongest at pulling a leader down.

House Ceres · the Bread-Bakers

The Tithe

Steal income · 2 turns
Rules
vs one player

The target's next Draft is cut by 50%, and the one after by 25%. The caster gains the same amounts on their own next two Drafts.

vs an alliance

Each member's next Draft is cut by 30%, then 15%. The caster collects the totals.

Gain cap v5

The caster gains at most 100 in total across the two Drafts. The target's cut has no cap.

Power vs one player (swing)
≈24Round 4 · 12 lost, 12 gained
≈80Round 11 · 40 lost (27 + 13), 40 gained (under the cap)
Desired outcome

The only Ultimate that makes the caster stronger. It narrows the reinforcement gap from both sides at once, over two turns.

Intended meta

Reverses the snowball: the bigger the leader's income, the bigger the theft. In 5METH, seat 3 earned 6 a turn. A Tithe on the 54-a-turn leader would have given it turns of 33 and 19.

House theme

Ceres feeds the valley, and the Tithe decides who eats. It's the harvest taken back from whoever grew fat on it.

“Bread first. Then we talk. Then I stab you.”Barley, Ceres Envoy

Make it stronger
  • Keep the second turn at 50%.
  • Add a third turn at 15%.
Make it weaker
  • The caster gains only half of what's taken.
  • Cap the gain at 15 per Draft.
The cap is a safety net. No cast in 4,500 earlier sim games gained more than 64, so a 100 cap rarely if ever triggers. It only stops a freak late swing against a 150-a-turn leader.

House Apollo · the Sun

Solar Flare

Defense debuff
Rules
vs one player v5

Until the start of the caster's next turn, the target is Glared: their highest defense die is −1 (both dice in v4), but only against attacks by the caster and the caster's allies. The caster is Radiant on the casting turn: +1 on their highest attack die.

vs an alliance v5

The same, applied to every member: highest defense die −1 against the caster's party, and +1 attack for the caster that turn.

House Diana · the Huntresses

The Wild Hunt

Go for the Standard
Rules
vs one player

Until the start of the caster's next turn, against attacks by the caster and the caster's allies only: the target's Standard loses its honor guard (5 phantoms → 0) and the target can't spring reaction cards. The Standard can't move, and the target can't Fortify on their next turn.

vs an alliance

The same for every member: Standard guards drop to 0 and reaction cards don't work against the caster's party, and no member can move their Standard or Fortify on their next turn.

Long Strike v5

Until the start of the caster's next turn, the caster and every ally can attack territories 2 spaces away, as many times as they like. It isn't limited to the hunted target.

Standard attack odds (12 committed vs 10 defenders)
61→88%Honor guard removed

Desired outcome

Give the caster a real shot at the leader's Standard. Winning hands over the leader's entire House: all of its territories and cards.

Intended meta

The assassination threat. Leaders have to keep a real army on their Standard instead of the 2 defenders Sloppy found on territory 38. With no Fortify, the target can't rush armies back to cover it. It's the riskiest Ultimate: a failed Standard attack still costs the caster their own House.

House theme

The Huntresses run their quarry down, and the hunt always ends at the heart. Here Diana doesn't ambush. It goes after the one piece that matters.

“Run, little deer.”Proctor Diana

Make it stronger
  • Also kill 3 real armies on the Standard's territory.
  • Any attacker can join the hunt, not just the caster's party.
Make it weaker
  • Reaction cards still work against the hunters. Now that reactions fire on any rival attack, this is the first thing to give back.
  • Long Strike for the caster only, not their allies.
Reach was Diana's bottleneck. In v4, bots had no stack next to the leader's Standard on at least 3 of every 4 turns they could have cast. Long Strike fixes that, and matches Diana's cards, which already carry Long Strike. A win still takes a whole House, so more casts means more eliminations.

SimLong Strike made the Hunt castable: 42 casts per 500 games as written and 27 with the deserter bar, up from 19 in v4. All 42 as-written casts took a House, and 26 of the 27 with the bar did. Decisions for .008

Settled on 2 October 2026. The rules on this sheet, as written, go into the engine in version .008. The build spec is docs/patch-008.md in the game folder.

1 · No deserter bar

The 2-turn alliance lockout ships alone. The sim's longer games (+11.2 turns, Siege at 61%) came from bots walking out of alliances to cast.

The shipped AI never leaves an alliance to become eligible. Whether human players do is the thing to watch in playtests.

2 · Jupiter's Draft cap

Only the targeted player's next Draft drops to 5. Other alliance members lose their stacks in the quadrant and keep their Draft.

3 · Diana's reaction cards

They stay blocked against the hunters. In a 1,500-game replay per variant, the hunted House held a reaction card in 18% of Hunts. Where it played one (11 Hunts), the hunters still took the House in 10, and the eleventh Hunt also failed with reactions blocked.

What decides a Hunt is Long Strike and the missing honor guard, not the reaction card.

4 · Cost and cooldown

3 cards (1+ from your birth House) and 3 of your own turns, unchanged.

Also in .008

House Ultimates are a War Settings switch, on by default and off for 2 Houses. Keeps get Walls (+1 on the highest defense die), and a neutral Keep no longer hands over its House's land. Players pick their House and choose their Primus from it, which replaces the Passage.

Still open after .008

Ultimates rein leaders in but don't change who is among the winners (56% vs 57% for the round-4 leader). If you want real upsets, the next lever is cast frequency: a cheaper cost or a shorter cooldown.
