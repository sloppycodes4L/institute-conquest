WHAT TO TRY
  • Zoom with the wheel or a pinch. Far out the map is political and armies are count plates; nearer, one champion a territory; close in, whole squads on real ground.
  • Draft: click your land to place armies. Attack: click a ringed territory, then a target: its odds are on its plate. Blitz it.
  • Cards: hover a tab in the tray (tap on a phone) to lift the card.
  • Houses, Regions, War Log: the three tabs on the right edge, or H, R and L.
  • Hold Alt for every name, region and land bridge. O sees through Olympus.
  • Watching shows a rival's turn: the camera follows and the dice roll beside the fight.
  • The sea is empty until an army crosses it. Every sea lane ends at a pier. In Attack, pick Bloodwater (the lake on your shore with a pier) and then Solar Scarp across the water: a galley in your colours takes the squad over, and its wake is as long as it is fast. Then Fortify ▸ from one to the other sends more across.
  • Zoom in on a Keep to see its Primus. Yours, of Mars, wears a wolf's pelt.
  • Lakes have shores: banks that come down from the land beside them, shallows, rushes, and the garrison's island on a neck of shingle. The Frostfangs' lakes are half frozen. Between the quadrants the ground has fallen away into rifts: rock walls, and a floor of boulders far below.
WHAT IS REAL
  • The valley is the game's own map (5 players, seed 7). The war on it is staged: round 6, you are House Mars.
  • Soldiers, Keeps and Olympus are built in code here, as stand-ins for modelled art. Menus are labels only.
