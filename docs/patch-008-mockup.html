Choices this mockup makes

These are the design calls behind the screens. Say which to change; everything else goes into the build as drawn.

The button is always thereScreens 06, 18

The Ultimate button sits in the Draft bar from turn 1 and says why it can't be used yet. The alternative is to hide it until a House is eligible, which is cleaner but teaches nothing.

One flow, four stepsScreens 07 to 11

Target, cards, picks, preview. Only Commit spends cards. Mars and Jupiter pick on the map; the other five Houses skip step 3.

The cut-off is drawnScreen 07

The target list ranks parties by Win % with a line under the last House that can't cast. It is the only place the standing appears; the Proctors' Book is unchanged.

Casts must be seenScreen 12

An Ultimate's announcement stays until you press Seen, like a rival's played card. The alternative is a timed banner, which is faster but easy to miss.

Icons under the bannerScreens 13, 19

Status icons get their own row under each House's stats and wrap when there are many. Hover or tap reads one out. The icon set is the design sheet's.

Pick, then confirmScreens 04, 05

Choose your Primus shows every Character at once and needs a second click to swear one in. Passives show at printed value.

House pills in the lobbyScreens 01 to 03

Each seat carries its House as a coloured pill. A House the host assigns is locked for that player until the host sets it back to Random.
